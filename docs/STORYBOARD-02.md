# Storyboard — No. 02, "How much is an hour of your attention worth?"

Five acts, three instruments, ~8 minutes. Surfaces: ink → parchment → night → parchment → parchment.

## Act I — The Receipt (ink)
**SEE** Two inputs (region chips; hours slider) and a receipt that prints itself line by line: issued to, attention supplied, quantity, revenue to Meta, per day, PRICE PER HOUR in oxblood display type, the minimum-wage comparison. The title reveals beside it; the subhead quotes the reader's own number.
**TOUCH** Change region or hours; every number in the piece follows. Share your receipt: a link carrying only the two inputs, which renders its own card.
**IDEA** The price exists, it is from the seller's own books, and it is tiny.

## Act II — The Market (parchment)
**SEE** Figure 1: 670 dots, one per five million daily people, filling as you scroll; one vermilion dot is the reader; three numbers alongside (people/day, revenue/day, your share). A ledger: 1833, 1971, 1973, 2024.
**IDEA** You are inventory, not a product; cheap things are sold in bulk, so the incentive is volume.

## Act III — The Panic (night)
**SEE** Figure 2: six bars around a zero axis, technology use as the vermilion reference; potatoes, glasses, marijuana, bullying arrive around it; sleep breaks the chart. Hover/keys read each.
**IDEA** The average association is tiny and unstable; the dispute is real; "the cause" is the panic's mistake.

## Act IV — The Trap (parchship)
**SEE** Three evidence rows with large numerals (22%, $100, 31%). Figure 3: a ten-by-ten grid of people; a slider for how many of yours stay; your best move flips; a checkbox lets everyone decide at once and shows the better world nobody can reach alone.
**IDEA** The harm the minutes can't show is between people: a collective trap, not a willpower failure.

## Act V — The Ledger Closes (parchment)
**SEE** Three prices for the same hour, in the reader's hours: what Meta earned, what it took to pay someone to leave, what students would pay to have it gone for all. Instrument 3: "If everyone you know left at once, what would you leave?" Coda. Share. Hand-off to No. 01.
**IDEA** The market priced your hour; you never did.

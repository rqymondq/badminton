# Storyboard — "How much of what you believe is actually yours?"

Target reading time: 6–8 minutes. Six acts. The surface alternates between ink
and parchment so the reader *feels* the shift between thinking and reading.

Legend — **SEE**: what is on screen · **SCROLL**: what changes with scroll ·
**TOUCH**: what the reader can interact with · **CUT**: the transition out ·
**IDEA**: what the visual is arguing.

---

## Act I — The Question (ink · pinned, ~300vh)

**SEE** An ink-black screen. A small mono label: `I · The Question`. Then one
line in Fraunces, revealed word by word with a clip mask, not a fade:
*"You did not begin with a blank mind."*

**SCROLL** Sixteen words of influence (Family, Language, Place, Class, Era,
Religion, School, Friends, Screens, Accident, Stories, Praise, Fear, Hunger,
Luck, Habit) enter from beyond the viewport edges in mixed sizes and weights.
As the reader scrolls they drift toward the centre and settle on an orbit
around an empty centre: the abstract worldview. Their opacities layer. At the
end of the orbit the centre resolves into the title, composed in four registers:
`How much` (small) / `of what you believe` (huge) / `is actually` (italic) /
`yours?` (vermilion).

**TOUCH** None. The opening is to be watched. It is short.

**CUT** The ring and the title remain; the ink surface cross-dissolves to
parchment as the dek slides up into the reading column.

**IDEA** A belief system is an accumulation before it is a choice.

---

## Act II — The Inheritance (parchment · scroll-linked figure)

**SEE** Running head *"Before we learn to question the world, we learn how to
see it."* Reading column on the left. On the right, **Figure 1**: an engraved
human profile in hatched linework, surrounded by eight influence nodes on an
arc: Family, Language, Place, Faith, School, Friends, Class, Media.

**SCROLL** As the figure enters, hairlines draw from each node to the figure
(stroke-dashoffset). Translucent discs of colour (each node's tint) grow over
the figure and overlap. The figure becomes layered, less legible, richer.

**TOUCH** Hover/focus/tap a node → a marginal card reveals a short example of
how that influence might shape a worldview, labelled *illustrative, not a
measurement*. Keyboard: nodes are buttons; arrow keys move between them.
Mobile: figure above, nodes as a horizontal strip, card below.

**CUT** Column text closes on: inherited beliefs are not therefore wrong —
their origins are worth examining. Surface darkens to ink across one viewport.

**IDEA** Development is layered and simultaneous, not a single cause.

---

## Act III — The Elephant and the Rider (ink · pinned, ~350vh)

**SEE** A large, slowly breathing vermilion mass (intuition) fills the
left-centre. A small lapis instrument — a thin ring with a cross-hair — floats
above it (reasoning). Heading: *"What if your reasons came after your
conclusions?"*

**SCROLL** Four beats, scrubbed:
1. A scenario appears in the margin (a harmless-taboo case from Haidt's 1993
   study, paraphrased).
2. The mass lurches — a fast, full-body reaction. The word `WRONG` flashes in
   the mass for a fraction of the scroll. The ring is dragged along.
3. Reasons type out in lapis, one at a time, each line drawn *from* the ring
   *to* the mass — after the fact.
4. A fourth beat: a reason line is drawn the other way, and the mass yields
   slightly. Caption: reasoning can steer, sometimes.

**TOUCH** **The Flash Judgement** — a small instrument below the scene. The
reader is shown a scenario, chooses *Wrong / Not wrong / Unsure*; the
instrument records only the time taken (locally, in memory) and then asks for
a reason. The result compares the two timings. Nothing is sent anywhere.

**CUT** The mass settles and dims to graphite; a hairline splits the screen
vertically, which becomes Act IV's divider.

**IDEA** Intuition first, strategic reasoning second — usually, not always.

---

## Act IV — Two Minds, One World (parchment split · interactive)

**SEE** A split screen. Left tinted vermilion, right tinted lapis. Centre: a
single scenario — *The Village Doctor* — written once. Below it, nine facts
about the case set in a single column.

**TOUCH** A slider (range input, keyboard-accessible) between *Equal rules*
and *Standing loyalty*. Moving it reweights the facts: those that matter to
each lens grow in size and opacity; the others recede. Each side's
interpretation — *what it notices, what it fears, what it values, where it is
vulnerable* — updates. The divider moves with the slider.

**SCROLL** Entering, the two tints wipe in from the sides. Nothing is pinned.

**CUT** The two tints desaturate into one parchment; the reading column notes
that both lenses are using the same facts.

**IDEA** Disagreement is often a difference in weighting, not in intelligence.

---

## Act V — The Search for Understanding (night · pinned, ~400vh)

**SEE** Deep ink with a lapis undertone, as a study at night. Four words in
Fraunces italic: *Philosophy · Law · Medicine · Theology* — Faust's four
faculties.

**SCROLL** The four fragments multiply: 4 → 16 → 64 → hundreds. Fragments are
short — disciplines, instruments, units, titles, Faust's own lines. They drift
in a slow parallax field. At peak density the field blurs into texture and a
single question emerges in the clear centre: *"Does knowing more necessarily
mean understanding more?"* A mono counter in the corner counts fragments, then
stops counting.

**TOUCH** None by design. The reader is meant to feel the weight.

**CUT** The fragments fall away with the scroll; the field lightens into dawn
parchment.

**IDEA** Faust's crisis was not too little knowledge. Ours may not be either.

---

## Act VI — The Unfinished Mind (dawn parchment · quiet interaction)

**SEE** The ring of influence words from Act I returns, now in ink on
parchment, legible, slowly turning. Heading: *"Perhaps thinking independently
doesn't mean thinking without influence."*

**TOUCH** A single ruled line: *"What is one belief you've never seriously
questioned?"* The reader can write. A note under it states plainly that
nothing is stored or sent. On *Keep it*, the reader's line joins the ring as
a node in vermilion — their word among the inherited ones. On *Let it go*, the
line dissolves.

**SCROLL** The ring eases to a stop. The closing thought appears, then the
sources, set as a proper bibliography with evidence registers.

**IDEA** Independence is not the absence of influence. It is examination.
